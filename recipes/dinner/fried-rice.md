# Cameroonian fried Rice with Chicken

**Category:** Dinner  
**Prep time:** 25 minutes  
**Cook time:** 45 minutes  
**Servings:** 4  
**Difficulty:** Medium  

A delicious one-pot rice dish cooked in a rich tomato and pepper sauce, infused with spices and served with tender, flavourful chicken.

---

## Ingredients

### For the rice
- 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
- 4 medium tomatoes
- 2 medium onions
- 1 red bell pepper
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 small piece of fresh ginger (about 2 cm)
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2½ cups chicken stock or water
- 1 tsp curry powder
- ½ tsp thyme
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 seasoning cube
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Salt to taste
- 1 bay leaf

### For the chicken
- 500 g chicken pieces
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 seasoning cube
- ½ tsp black or white pepper
- ½ tsp paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- Salt to taste
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil

---

## Instructions

### Preparing the chicken

1. Wash the chicken pieces and place them in a cooking pot.
2. Add the chopped onion, seasoning cube, pepper, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
3. Add approximately ½ cup of water, cover the pot and cook over medium heat for 15–20 minutes, or until the chicken is almost completely cooked.
4. Remove the chicken from the pot and reserve the stock for cooking the rice.
5. Heat 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil in a frying pan and fry the chicken pieces for approximately 5–7 minutes, turning occasionally until golden brown. Set aside.

### Preparing the rice

6. Wash the rice thoroughly under running water until the water becomes relatively clear. Drain and set aside.
7. Blend the tomatoes, red bell pepper, one onion, garlic and ginger until smooth.
8. Heat 3 tablespoons of vegetable oil in a large cooking pot over medium heat.
9. Finely chop the remaining onion and add it to the hot oil. Fry for approximately 2 minutes until softened.
10. Add the tomato paste and stir continuously for 3–4 minutes to remove its raw taste.
11. Pour in the blended tomato mixture and cook uncovered for 12–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the mixture thickens and most of the excess water evaporates.
12. Add the curry powder, thyme, paprika, black pepper, seasoning cube, bay leaf and salt. Stir well.
13. Pour in the reserved chicken stock and enough additional water to make approximately 2½ cups of liquid in total.
14. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil, then add the washed rice and stir until everything is evenly combined.
15. Cover the pot tightly and reduce the heat to low. Allow the rice to cook for approximately 25–30 minutes.
16. Check the rice occasionally. If it is still hard and the liquid has dried up, add a little hot water, cover and continue cooking until the rice is tender.
17. Add the fried chicken pieces on top of the rice during the final 5 minutes of cooking.
18. Once the rice is fully cooked, turn off the heat and allow it to rest, covered, for 5 minutes before serving.

---

## Notes

- **Avoid mushy rice:** Do not add too much water. The quantity needed may vary depending on the type of rice.
- **For extra flavour:** Add chopped carrots, green beans or green peas.
- **For a smoky flavour:** Allow the rice to cook slightly longer over low heat so that a light crust forms at the bottom. Avoid letting it burn.
- **Storage:** Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days. Reheat thoroughly before eating.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve with fried plantains, fresh cucumber slices or a simple vegetable salad.

---

## About This Recipe

fried rice is one of West Africa's most popular rice dishes, with different countries having their own variations and cooking traditions. This Cameroonian-inspired version combines locally familiar spices, fresh tomatoes and tender chicken to create a satisfying dinner that can be enjoyed with family and friends.
